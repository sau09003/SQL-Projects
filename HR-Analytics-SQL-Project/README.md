## 📌 Project Overview
This project is a **complete HR Analytics solution built using SQL**.  
It focuses on analyzing employee data to extract meaningful business insights related to:

- Employee attrition (turnover)
- Salary distribution
- Job satisfaction
- Work-life balance
- Employee performance
- Department and job role analysis

The goal is to help HR teams make **data-driven decisions** to improve employee retention and workforce management.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- SQL (MySQL)
- Database: HRAnalyticsDB

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

Table Name: `EmployeeData`

Key Columns:
- EmployeeNumber
- Age
- Gender
- Department
- JobRole
- MonthlyIncome
- Attrition
- OverTime
- JobSatisfaction
- WorkLifeBalance
- PerformanceRating
- TotalWorkingYears
- YearsAtCompany

---

## 📊 Project Objectives

- Understand employee distribution across departments
- Analyze salary structure across roles
- Identify key factors affecting attrition
- Measure employee satisfaction and workload impact
- Build reusable SQL reports and dashboards
- Use advanced SQL techniques for deeper insights

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Employee Overview
- Total employees count
- Gender distribution
- Age statistics (min, max, average)
- Department and job role distribution

---

### 2. Salary Analysis
- Average salary by department and job role
- Top 10 highest-paid employees
- Employees earning above company average

---

### 3. Attrition Analysis
- Overall attrition rate
- Attrition by department
- Attrition by job role
- Attrition vs overtime
- Attrition vs salary
- Attrition vs job satisfaction

---

### 4. Work Environment Analysis
- Job satisfaction by department
- Work-life balance impact on attrition
- High-risk employee identification

---

### 5. Advanced SQL Analysis
- Window functions (RANK, ROW_NUMBER)
- Top 3 salaries per department
- Running salary totals
- Employees above department average salary

---

### 6. HR KPIs & Reporting
- Retention rate
- Attrition rate
- Average salary
- Average tenure
- Department-wise and role-wise analysis

---

## 🚨 Key Business Insights

- Employees working overtime have higher attrition rates
- Low salary employees are more likely to leave
- Poor work-life balance significantly increases attrition
- Job satisfaction is a strong predictor of retention
- Certain departments show higher turnover rates

This project helps HR teams to:

- Optimize salary structure
